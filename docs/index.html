Sources

Three libraries, one chooser

Each source is its own library with its own artwork, stats and hub. They stack into a chooser — and Beam reopens the one you used last.

Mega. Sign in once; your cloud becomes a poster wall. An embedded engine serves files directly — nothing downloads first.
Seedbox (HTTPS). Point Beam at any HTTPS file server for a second cloud library with its own isolated engine — a misbehaving box never touches Mega playback.
Local drives. USB and internal storage with per-drive Movies/TV assignment. Incremental scans; detached drives are remembered and flagged, not forgotten.

Beam Mobile · Phone & tablet

Your library, in your pocket

A phone and tablet app built on Beam on your TV. Same library, same artwork, same watch history: browse it from the sofa, play it on the phone, send it to the big screen or take it with you. It talks only to your TV over your home network, with no account, no cloud, and no keys or passwords stored on the phone.

The same library, in your hand

Posters, backdrops, title logos, ratings, cast and credits from your TV. Home brings Continue watching, Next up, top-rated and genre rows.

Find anything

Movie and TV grids with search, sorting by title, newest, IMDb rating or recently watched, and Unwatched, In progress, genre and Downloaded filters.

Play it here

A built-in player streams the original file from your TV, and the next episode starts by itself. Resume points and watched marks sync both ways.

Send it to the TV

Tap On TV and the title starts on the big screen. A remote bar appears with play/pause, a seek bar, −10/+30 s, previous/next episode and stop.

Download for offline

Download movies, single episodes or whole seasons, and pause, resume or retry them. Titles, details and art stay browsable without the TV, and progress made away from home syncs back when you return.

Cast to any TV

Downloads cast to Chromecast and Google TV, to Samsung, LG and other DLNA smart TVs, or to Beam Receiver. The phone serves the file itself over your Wi-Fi.

Casting that just plays

MKV files are repackaged on the fly without re-encoding. Audio a Chromecast can't play (DTS, TrueHD, AC-3, E-AC-3) can be converted to AAC, and H.265 converted to H.264 for older Chromecasts. Each conversion is a switch in Settings.

A Kids profile

With Kids Mode on, "Who's watching?" opens at every launch. Kids get their own bright look and only the titles picked for them on the TV: no remote, no settings, just Play.

Your player, your call

Use the built-in player, choose each time, or always open a favourite app such as VLC, mpv or MX Player. If the phone can't decode a file, Beam Mobile offers to open it in an external player.

Beam Receiver · Android TV

Any Android TV, ready to cast to

A small standalone app for Android TV, Google TV and NVIDIA SHIELD. Install it once and the TV appears in Beam Mobile's Cast list. Your phone's downloads then play on the big screen through Beam's own player.

Shows up by itself. Answers network discovery as "Living Room TV (Beam)", so it is never mistaken for the TV's built-in receiver, and rejoins after Wi-Fi or Ethernet changes.
Always ready. Runs quietly from boot. A video sent while the TV sleeps wakes the screen and opens over whatever is on; Back returns you to it.
Beam's picture pipeline. Dolby Vision profile 7 remuxes fall back to their HDR10 base layer instead of a black screen. HDR switches on even when a file's colour tags are missing, and a 128 MB buffer rides out Wi-Fi dips at remux bitrates.
Sound stays intact. Audio passes through to your AV receiver or soundbar as the TV allows. There's no refresh-rate switching, so no HDMI resync drops the sound at the start, and DTS and TrueHD can be decoded on the TV as a backup.
Controls you know. Beam's player look: title, spec chips, a timebar, −5/+15 s, subtitles and audio tracks. The phone's remote bar drives it too: play, pause, seek and stop.

Sharing

A media server for Kodi

One toggle publishes the whole library — movies and TV with covers and titles — as a UPnP/DLNA media server. Kodi or any UPnP client browses Beam and direct-plays from Mega, your seedbox and local drives, with Beam's engines relaying the original bytes. Survives reboots until you switch it off.

Engine

Under the hood

Every source looks identical to the decoder, profiled on real hardware until cold starts, seeks and steady-state all held up.

Embedded engine

Runs as a local server; the player streams from 127.0.0.1, so cloud, seedbox and USB all look identical to the decoder.

Tuned RAM read-ahead

A 128 MB player buffer over hundreds of MB of engine read-ahead, profiled until a memory-constrained 2015 SHIELD held smooth.

Dual engines

A tuned stable engine by default, with a one-toggle experimental engine for A/B testing on your own device.

Room-backed catalog

Library, watch state and specs in a relational store; the app starts from cache instantly and scans refresh incrementally.
