Setup

Connect it to your TV

Optional. Beam Mobile reads the library from Beam on your TV, so the TV does the setup and the phone just connects. Android 8 or newer, on the same Wi-Fi as the TV.

  1. On the TV, in Beam: Settings → Network Sharing → Web Dashboard → on. This gives the phone the library, the remote and watched sync.
  2. Also turn on Media Server for Kodi (UPnP). The phone needs it to play and download Mega and seedbox titles, and to find the TV by itself. Local-drive titles also play with Share Local Library (Beam Link) on.
  3. On the phone, open Beam Mobile → Settings → TV connection and tap Find TV, or type the address Beam shows under its Settings and tap Connect.
  4. Optional: Settings → Casting → Cast to TVs adds Cast to downloaded titles. Convert audio and Convert video help older Chromecasts.
Note  Settings → Kids Mode on the phone adds a "Who's watching?" screen. The Kids profile shows the titles picked for Kids on the TV; picking stays on the TV.
